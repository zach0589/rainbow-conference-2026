What This Means for Your Funnel

  • The bottom of the funnel still matters most. LSA and PPC are where leads come from. But costs are up 20.9% — the bottom alone isn't enough anymore.
  • The top of the funnel makes the bottom work harder. Branded searches convert at 6x the rate of generic terms. Awareness doesn't replace demand capture — it supercharges it.
  • AI is the new front door. 41% of your potential customers are using AI to research you. Your content, reviews, and GBP feed what AI recommends.
Keep investing in the bottom. But the operators pulling ahead are the ones adding layers above it.

Where Does Your Marketing Fit?

Map each activity to one of these four stages.

Step 1: Map What You're Doing

  • Write down every marketing activity you're currently doing
  • For each one, answer: WHY are you doing this?
  • Then answer: WHERE does it fit in the funnel?
  • Don't overthink it. Just get it all on paper.

Step 2: Find the Gaps

  • Which funnel stages have the most activity? Which have none?
  • Heavy on Decision (LSA, PPC) but light on everything else?
  • No Awareness activity at all?
  • Nothing in Conversion beyond "we answer the phone"?
  • Circle the biggest gap — that's your #1 priority

Step 3: Pick Your Next Move

  • Based on your gaps, choose 1-3 things you want to add to your marketing
  • For each one, write down: What is it?
  • Where does it fit in the funnel?
  • Why this one?
  • Don't worry about the "how" yet — that's what we're here for
