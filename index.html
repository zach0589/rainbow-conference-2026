What Is a Media Mix?

  • Your media mix is the combination of marketing channels you invest in and how they work together
  • A strong media mix covers three layers:

Layer 1: Demand Capture

Catching people actively searching

  • LSA
  • PPC
  • SEO
  • GBP

Layer 2: Demand Creation

Building awareness before the emergency

  • Retargeting
  • Social media
  • YouTube
  • Community involvement

Layer 3: Trust & Conversion

Turning leads into booked jobs

  • Reviews
  • Website
  • Speed to lead
  • Follow-up
Most restoration companies invest heavily in Layer 1 and almost nothing in Layers 2 and 3.

The Marketing Funnel

Awareness

  • Social media
  • Community sponsorships
  • YouTube brand ads
  • Vehicle wraps / yard signs

Consideration

  • Retargeting ads
  • Blog content / SEO
  • YouTube how-to content
  • Email nurture

Decision

  • Local Service Ads
  • Google Ads (PPC)
  • SEO (high-intent keywords)
  • Google Business Profile

Conversion

  • Website experience
  • Online reviews
  • Call answering / speed to lead
  • Follow-up systems / CRM
Restoration is emergency-driven. The decision stage lasts 5 minutes. Trust has to be built BEFORE the emergency.

AI Is Changing How Customers Find You

What's Happening

  • 90% of consumers engage with AI summaries when searching
  • 41% of restoration customers use ChatGPT after seeing an ad
  • Google AI Overviews push organic results further down the page
  • Consumers are searching conversationally — longer, more specific queries

What This Means for Your Funnel

  • Brand recognition matters more — AI recommends brands it "knows"
  • Reviews and GBP carry more weight in AI-generated results
  • Content (blogs, FAQs) feeds the AI answers about your business
  • Paid search becomes more important as organic real estate shrinks
AI doesn't replace your marketing funnel — it makes every stage more competitive. The operators who invest across the full funnel will adapt. The ones relying on a single channel will get squeezed.

What This All Means for Your Funnel

  • Decision stage is getting more expensive. LSA costs up 20.9%. You can't just outspend your way to leads.
  • Awareness drives the cheapest conversions. 29% of homeowners already have a name in mind when they search. Brand building pays off at the bottom.
  • Reviews are non-negotiable. 31.5% say a business needs at least 15 recent reviews. 35% are positively influenced when you respond to negative reviews.
  • AI is the new front door. 41% of your potential customers are using AI tools to research you. Your content, reviews, and GBP feed what AI says about you.
  • The operators who cover all four funnel stages are the ones growing.

WORKSHOP

Build Your Marketing Funnel

Step 1: Map What You're Doing

  • Write down every marketing activity you're currently doing
  • For each one, answer two questions:
    • WHY are you doing this? (What's the goal?)
    • WHERE does it fit in the funnel? (Awareness, Consideration, Decision, or Conversion)
  • Don't overthink it. Just get it all on paper.

Step 2: Find the Gaps

  • Look at your funnel map
  • Which stages have the most activity? Which have none?
  • Common patterns we see:
    • Heavy on Decision (LSA, PPC) — light on everything else
    • No Awareness activity at all
    • Nothing in Conversion beyond "we answer the phone"
  • Circle the biggest gap — that's your #1 priority
