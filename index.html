The Marketing Funnel

Awareness

Consideration

Retargeting, blog content, SEO, email nurture

Decision

LSA, Google Ads, SEO (high-intent), GBP

Conversion

Website, reviews, speed to lead, follow-up

Restoration is emergency-driven. The consideration phase is far shorter than most industries. Trust has to be built BEFORE the emergency.
Here's how those three layers map to the marketing funnel. At the top — Awareness — that's social media, sponsorships, your truck wraps, community events. People learn you exist before they need you. Consideration — retargeting, blog content, SEO. They've got a problem and they're comparing options. Decision — LSA, Google Ads, GBP. They're searching right now and picking who to call. Conversion — your website, your reviews, your speed to lead. They clicked or called — now they need to become a booked job. Here's what makes restoration different from most industries: when a pipe bursts at 2 AM, the consideration phase barely exists. They're not shopping around for a week. Which means the trust has to already be there. If they don't know your name before the emergency, you're competing purely on who shows up first in the search results.

Where Does Your Marketing Fit?

Map each activity to one of these four stages.

Keep this slide up briefly as a reference while they start Step 1. "Here's your funnel. Every marketing activity you write down should land in one of these four stages. Use this as your guide." Then advance to Step 1.

Step 1: Map What You're Doing

  • Write down every marketing activity you're currently doing
  • For each one, answer: WHY are you doing this?
  • Then answer: WHERE does it fit in the funnel?
  • Don't overthink it. Just get it all on paper.
~10 MINUTES. "Write down everything you're spending time or money on for marketing. LSA, SEO, social media, truck wraps, sponsorships — all of it. Then for each one, answer why you're doing it and where it fits in the funnel." Walk the room. Help people who are stuck. Common prompt: "Think about what you did last month — where did you spend money? Where did you spend time?" Watch for people who can't answer WHY — that's a coaching moment.

Step 2: Find the Gaps

  • Which funnel stages have the most activity? Which have none?
  • Heavy on Decision (LSA, PPC) but light on everything else?
  • No Awareness activity at all?
  • Nothing in Conversion beyond "we answer the phone"?
  • Circle the biggest gap — that's your #1 priority
~10 MINUTES. "Now look at what you've got. Where did everything cluster? I'll bet most of you have a lot in Decision and not much anywhere else." Walk the room. Call out patterns you're seeing: "Raise your hand if Awareness is your emptiest stage." (Most will.) "That's normal — and that's your opportunity." Help them rate 1-5 and identify their biggest gap. The gap identification is the key moment — this is what sets up Step 3.

Step 3: Pick Your Next Move

  • Based on your gaps, choose 1-3 things you want to add to your marketing
  • For each one, write down: What is it?
  • Where does it fit in the funnel?
  • Why this one?
  • Don't worry about the "how" yet — that's what we're here for
~10 MINUTES. "Now that you know your gaps, pick 1-3 things you want to add. Not things you're already doing — new things. Write down what it is, where it fits in the funnel, and why you picked it." This is where the SOS team should be actively helping at tables. Don't sell — coach. Ask "what gap are you trying to fill?" and help them think through what tactic fits. The "don't worry about the how" line is your natural bridge to conversations about working together. LAST 10 MINUTES: Group discussion. Ask the room: "Who found everything clustered in one stage?" "Who had a gap they didn't expect?" "Who picked something for Awareness they've never done before?" Get 3-4 people to share.
