What Is a Media Mix?

  • The combination of marketing channels you invest in and how they work together
  • It defines where you show up, how you show up, and how often
  • A strong media mix balances three things:
    • Capturing demand that already exists
    • Creating demand before emergencies happen
    • Converting attention into booked jobs
  • No two markets require the exact same mix
Key idea: Stop thinking about channels. Start thinking about systems. A channel is a tactic. A system is a strategy.

The Three Layers of a Strong Media Mix

1. Demand Capture

Bottom of Funnel

  • Local Service Ads
  • Google Ads (PPC)
  • SEO for emergency searches
  • Google Business Profile

2. Demand Creation

Middle / Top of Funnel

  • Retargeting ads
  • YouTube pre-roll
  • Social media content
  • Community involvement

3. Trust & Conversion

The Revenue Layer

  • Reviews & reputation
  • Website speed & UX
  • Speed to lead
  • Follow-up & CRM
Most restoration companies invest heavily in Layer 1 and almost nothing in Layers 2 and 3. The best operators cover all three.

The Marketing Funnel

1. Awareness

People learn you exist. They see your brand before they need you.

2. Consideration

They have a problem and are comparing options. Reading reviews, checking websites.

3. Decision

They're ready to call someone. Searching "water damage restoration near me" right now.

4. Conversion

They clicked, called, or submitted a form. Now they need to become a booked, completed job.

Restoration is different. When a pipe bursts at 2 AM, the decision stage lasts 5 minutes. Trust has to be built before the emergency. The company that shows up first usually wins.

Where Each Channel Fits in the Funnel

Awareness

  • Social media (organic and paid)
  • Community sponsorships and events
  • YouTube brand ads
  • Vehicle wraps and yard signs

Consideration

  • Retargeting ads
  • Blog content and SEO
  • YouTube how-to content

Decision

  • Local Service Ads
  • Google Ads (PPC)
  • SEO (high-intent keywords)
  • Google Business Profile

Conversion

  • Website experience (speed, mobile)
  • Online reviews and reputation
  • Call answering and speed to lead
  • Follow-up systems and CRM

AI Is Changing the Game

What's Happening

  • Google AI Overviews now appear on many service searches
  • Organic results are being pushed further down the page
  • AI-generated content is flooding competitor blogs
  • Consumers are starting to search differently -- conversational, longer queries
  • Voice search and AI assistants are growing in home emergencies

What This Means for You

  • SEO is still critical but the rules are shifting
  • Quality, original content matters more than ever
  • Google Business Profile and reviews carry more weight in AI results
  • Paid search (LSA + PPC) becomes even more important as organic real estate shrinks
  • The companies with strong brands will be the ones AI recommends
The takeaway: AI doesn't replace your marketing funnel -- it makes every stage more competitive. The operators who invest across the full funnel will adapt. The ones relying on a single channel will get squeezed.

What This Data Means for Your Funnel

  • Decision stage is getting more expensive. LSA costs up 20.9%, more competitors every quarter. You can't just outspend your way to leads anymore.
  • Awareness drives the cheapest conversions. Branded searches convert at 28% vs 4.68% for generic terms. Every dollar in brand building pays off at the bottom of the funnel.
  • The Conversion layer is where money is made or lost. Operators with 50+ reviews and sub-5-minute response times consistently achieve the lowest CPLs.
  • AI is compressing organic visibility. Paid channels and strong GBP profiles are now table stakes, not optional.
  • The operators who cover all four funnel stages are the ones growing. Everyone else is fighting over the same shrinking space.

The Most Common Mistakes We See

  • Over-relying on LSA as the only lead source
  • No retargeting -- letting website visitors leave and forget you
  • A website that looks outdated or loads slowly on mobile
  • Not answering calls within 3 rings or returning missed calls within 15 min
  • No system for tracking which channels actually produce revenue
  • Spending money without knowing your cost per booked job
  • Ignoring reviews or not actively generating them
The gap between the best and worst is not about budget. The wide CPL distribution ($44 to $325 for LSA alone) reflects differences in optimization, relevance, and operational responsiveness -- not just spend.

What the Best Operators Do Differently

  • They invest across all three layers, not just demand capture
  • They track performance from first click to completed job
  • They treat their website and reviews as marketing assets, not afterthoughts
  • They answer the phone fast and follow up faster
  • They adjust their mix quarterly based on data, not gut feel
  • They build brand recognition so people search their name, not just their service
Remember: Branded searches convert at 28%. Generic searches convert under 5%. The best operators are playing a long game -- and the data shows it pays off.

WORKSHOP

Build Your Marketing Funnel

Workshop Goal

  • We are NOT teaching marketing theory
  • We ARE mapping YOUR specific marketing right now
  • By the end of this workshop, you will:
    • See your full marketing funnel on paper
    • Identify the gaps in your current approach

Step 1: Map What You're Doing

  • Write down every marketing activity you're currently doing
  • For each one, answer two questions:
    • WHY are you doing this? (What's the goal?)
    • WHERE does it fit in the funnel? (Awareness, Consideration, Decision, or Conversion)
  • Don't overthink it. Just get it all on paper.

Step 2: Find the Gaps

  • Look at your funnel map
  • Which stages have the most activity? Which have none?
  • Common patterns we see:
    • Heavy on Decision (LSA, PPC) -- light on everything else
    • No Awareness activity at all
    • Nothing in Conversion beyond "we answer the phone"
  • Circle the biggest gap -- that's your #1 priority
