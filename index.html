The Reality Right Now

  • Google still handles 90% of search traffic
  • AI traffic converts at 5x the rate
  • 41% of restoration customers already use AI to research
  • Less volume, but much higher quality traffic

The Good News

  • Google and AI reward the same thing
  • Authoritative content on your website
  • Strong reviews and a managed GBP
  • A brand worth citing — AI recommends what it "knows"
You don't need two strategies. Authoritative content, strong reviews, a brand worth citing. Google and AI are two systems rewarding the same thing.

What This Means for Your Funnel

  • Decision is getting expensive. LSA costs up 20.9%. You can't outspend your way to leads.
  • Awareness drives the cheapest conversions. 29% already have a name in mind when they search.
  • Reviews are non-negotiable. 31.5% need 15+ recent reviews to be influenced. 35% respond positively when you reply to negatives.
  • AI is the new front door. 41% use AI to research you. Your content, reviews, and GBP feed what AI recommends.
  • Operators who cover all four funnel stages are the ones growing.

WORKSHOP

Build Your Marketing Funnel

Where Does Your Marketing Fit?

Map each activity to one of these four stages.

Step 1: Map What You're Doing

  • Write down every marketing activity you're currently doing
  • For each one, answer two questions:
    • WHY are you doing this? What's the goal?
    • WHERE does it fit? (Awareness, Consideration, Decision, or Conversion)
  • Don't overthink it. Just get it all on paper.

Step 2: Find the Gaps

  • Which funnel stages have the most activity? Which have none?
  • Common patterns:
    • Heavy on Decision (LSA, PPC) — light on everything else
    • No Awareness activity at all
    • Nothing in Conversion beyond "we answer the phone"
  • Circle the biggest gap — that's your #1 priority
